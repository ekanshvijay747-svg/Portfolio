Wingmate: Air Cadet Hub

Wingmate is a mobile application I developed to centralize uniform regulations and aviation theory for the Air Cadet program. I spent the summer building this prototype to address the gap between scattered government manuals and practical application. The app provides offline video guides and a structured layout so cadets can easily locate uniform standards like badge placement and boot polishing. Changing the way cadets consume this knowledge helps the squadron improve standards through accessible design rather than forced memorization.

DeltaLog: Error Ledger

I built this custom error ledger to track and categorize my mistakes for the AMC 10 and competitive mathematics. Instead of simply studying harder, I used this system to log specific divergence points and cognitive blindspots. By tracking these trends over time, I discovered that my biggest contributors to lost points were perceptual misreads and calculation slips caused by rushing. Logging these specific errors allowed me to strategically adjust my test-taking process.

Process Optimization

This is the AMC 10 Pre-Flight Checklist I designed to optimize my exam execution. I realized that my mock test scores stalled not because of a lack of math skills, but due to an unstructured approach to solving problems. I implemented a strict routine, including reading questions twice, constraint logging, and variable normalization. These process-driven changes, combined with 5:00 AM study blocks, significantly improved my performance by eliminating unforced errors.

VEX V5 Drivetrain CAD

I taught myself CAD to prototype our VEX V5 robot before committing to a physical build, as metal parts are costly to fix once cut. I modeled the drivetrain over the summer to determine exact gear ratios and motor layouts ahead of time. Documenting the physical topology and sizing constraints gave our team a significant head-start, allowing us to have a fully functional drivetrain running within a week of the robotics lab opening.

VEX Engineering Notebook

This section of our Engineering Notebook documents our weekly deliverables and subsystem power budgets.
